BREADCRUMB

1. Drag Breadcrumb into Applications and open it.
2. Look for Breadcrumb in the menu bar. Click Start to begin recording.
3. Allow Screen Recording when asked. Accessibility is optional and adds window context.
4. Open Dashboard to explore your local time, apps, activities and projects.

Breadcrumb works without an account or internet connection. Sign in from Settings only if you want to sync with a portal.

Captures are readable local files. Pause or exclude apps before private work. History follows your chosen retention and storage limits. Recording pauses when storage is full.

To uninstall, quit Breadcrumb and move it to Trash. Your history is kept.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
